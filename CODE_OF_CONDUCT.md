# Code of Conduct

Nudge is a small project, and everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests or discussions) should feel welcome.

## Be kind

- Be friendly and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Respect different opinions and experience levels.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully.

## Not okay

- Insults, harassment, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Posting someone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, trolling, or anything else that makes the space unpleasant.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (the **···** menu), or [report the user to GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block anyone who breaks this code of conduct.

This code of conduct is based on the ideas of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
